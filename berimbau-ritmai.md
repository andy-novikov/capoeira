## Garsai / Sounds

| Skiemuo | Kaip išgauti | How to play |
|---|---|---|
| **či** | Akmenukas lengvai liečia stygą, cabaça prispausta prie pilvo | Stone lightly touches the string, cabaça pressed to the belly |
| **ton** | Akmenukas neliečia stygos, cabaça neliečia pilvo | Stone off the string, cabaça away from the belly (open) |
| **tin** | Akmenukas prispaustas prie stygos, cabaça neliečia pilvo | Stone pressed to the string, cabaça away from the belly (closed) |
| **tonš** | Akmenukas neliečia stygos, po to lengvai liečia stygą | Stone off the string, then lightly touches it |
| **ščik** | Caxixi purtymas | Shake the caxixi |

Hyphens (`ton-ton-ton`) join quick strokes; wider gaps in the original separate phrases (shown here as ` · `).
